# Golden scrapbook layout

Use this whenever the user wants the previously proven high-quality illustrated recipe-card style.

## Required visual anatomy

- Portrait long card on warm cream/parchment paper.
- Thin reddish-brown stitched/dashed outer border and a rounded inner frame.
- Compact title zone with a large rounded handwritten Chinese title and a short subtitle for serving/kcal/texture.
- A large central or upper-middle watercolor food hero inside its own rounded stitched frame. The dish is the visual focus; no text may cover it.
- Lower half uses a deliberate two-column system:
  - left: separate ingredient, sauce, tools, optional items, and tips paper boxes;
  - right: clearly numbered `STEP 1`–`STEP 6` cards, each with 1–2 concise action lines and a small operation illustration.
- Independent bottom/right cloud, stamp, or postal badge for serving/yield/kcal.
- Small tape, hearts, stars, arrows, doodles, and food icons may decorate empty space but cannot obstruct text.

## Palette and type

Use cream, beige, caramel, warm brown, reddish brown, soft orange, and small pastel accents. Avoid cold grey/blue tech styling, pure black slabs, thin Song/Ming typography, commercial-photo backgrounds, or sterile infographic grids.

Chinese should feel rounded, hand-lettered, and readable. Decorative title lettering may be playful; ingredient and step copy must remain clear.

## Content density

The card is rich but segmented. Keep every ingredient group and actionable step in its own container. If the content does not fit at readable size, create two cards—never compress everything into tiny text.

## Hard failures

Reject and regenerate if the output is any of the following:

- generic warm poster or PPT;
- bottom 2×3 six-grid;
- a small photo surrounded by text rather than a large watercolor hero;
- ingredients and steps flattened into three generic information panels;
- missing left ingredient/tools/tips column;
- unclear STEP labels or repeated/merged steps;
- incomplete/clipped kcal badge;
- text covering food;
- excessive slogans or internal evidence labels;
- gibberish, truncation, collision, or bottom-edge clipping.

## Reference asset

When supported, use `assets/golden-reference-day1.png` as an image-edit/style reference. Preserve its design grammar, not its original dish name or text.
